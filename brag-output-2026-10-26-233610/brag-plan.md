# Brag Plan: AUGMENT Context OS

## What is this app?
AUGMENT is a research-to-action workspace that turns a mission into a source-grounded brief, saved context, and next actions.

## The angle
Treat the product like a calm, precise operating system booting into focus. A cursor drives the viewer through the actual mission flow: start a mission, attach context, watch the brief resolve into cited evidence and actions.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black system boot screen. The red live loading line races across while the phrase “MAKE THE NEXT MOVE CLEAR.” locks in.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Cursor zooms into the Mission Builder and types a real research mission.
- Cursor hovers across the three operating modes: VISION / NEURAL / MOTION.
- Source chips and a research progress line resolve into the AUGMENT BRIEF with cited source trail and next actions.

## Outro / punchline
The cursor clicks SAVE CASE. The system closes on “RESEARCH → CONTEXT → DECISION → NEXT ACTION” and “AUGMENT / CONTEXT OS”.

## User flow worth showing
Mission input → add source context and choose a mode → run research → receive cited brief and next actions → save the case.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium MacBook-style product film for a calm research operating system
- Interpretation: restrained dark UI, deliberate zooms, readable labels, and crisp cursor interactions rather than noisy effects.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 15 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #030303 / #080808
- Accent: red-400 / #f87171
- Text: white / #ffffff and gray-400 / #9ca3af
- Display font: JetBrains Mono
- Body font: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: black mission workspace with red progress line, source trail, and MacBook boot treatment.

## Share copy (draft)
Built AUGMENT: a calm research-to-action workspace that turns a mission into cited evidence, saved context, and the next useful move.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents with a warm electronic bed.
- Music: short generated synth pulse, because the external bundled track was not installed locally.
- Music treatment: fade in under the boot, lift on the brief reveal, fade under the final lockup.
- Music cue guidance: major cues at 2.4s, 7.6s, and 13.2s; cursor interactions use restrained ticks.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the red core glow breathes with the music bed.
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: type-in ticks, hover ticks for operating modes, brief confirmation hit on SAVE CASE.
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no distracting waveform, no claims beyond the product UI.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Boot / hook — 2.5s
Dark boot screen with live red loading line and “MAKE THE NEXT MOVE CLEAR.” Cursor enters from the lower right and aims at the workspace.
Sequential/interaction: cursor enters; loading line fills.
Audio intent: low electronic pulse and one soft system tick.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Mission Builder — 3.5s
Zoom into the real Mission Builder surface. Cursor clicks the mission field; “Compare the best stack for launching my research product” types in. The cursor hovers VISION, NEURAL, then MOTION, leaving VISION selected.
Sequential/interaction: yes — click, type, hover three mode cards.
Audio intent: subtle key ticks and three quiet hover accents.
Transition mood: soft push → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Source pipeline — 3.5s
Cursor clicks the source field and adds two source chips. The progress line advances through INGEST, VALIDATE, RETURN. Callout labels appear: “LIVE SOURCES”, “CITED OUTPUT”, “HUMAN-APPROVED”.
Sequential/interaction: yes — source chips arrive one by one, progress fills.
Audio intent: rising pulse with a restrained confirmation tick.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Cited brief / save — 3.5s
The AUGMENT BRIEF resolves: mission summary, KEY FINDINGS, SOURCE TRAIL, and NEXT ACTIONS. Cursor hovers a finding, then clicks SAVE CASE.
Sequential/interaction: yes — brief sections reveal in order; cursor clicks save.
Audio intent: brief lift, one confirmation hit.
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Lockup — 2.0s
Saved case state and product lockup: “RESEARCH → CONTEXT → DECISION → NEXT ACTION”. Cursor rests on the red core.
Sequential/interaction: none beyond cursor settle.
Audio intent: short final resolve and fade.
Transition mood: none

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic electronic / polished
**Audio summary:** a quiet system pulse builds through the research run, then resolves on the saved case.
